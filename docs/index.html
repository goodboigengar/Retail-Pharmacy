Retail Pharmacy Consultation Tool

Search the drug inventory, select medications for dispensing, and generate a patient consultation covering the components required under California Code of Regulations, Title 16, §1707.2. Works fully offline once installed.

Install this app

Open this page on the phone you want to install it on, then follow the on-screen steps to add it to the home screen.

You're in Safari — installing takes about 10 seconds:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down the menu that pops up and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.

A new app icon appears on your Home Screen — tap it any time to open the app, even with no internet connection.

Don't see "Add to Home Screen" in step 2?

That means this page is actually open inside another app's built-in browser (Messages, Mail, Slack, etc.), even though it looks like Safari. To fix it:

  1. Tap Copy Link below.
  2. Open the Safari app yourself — the blue compass icon on your Home Screen.
  3. Tap the address bar at the top, paste the link, and go.
  4. Then repeat steps 1–3 above.
