Retail Pharmacy Consultation Tool

Search the drug inventory, select medications for dispensing, and generate a patient consultation covering the components required under California Code of Regulations, Title 16, §1707.2. Works fully offline once installed.

Install this app

Open this page on the phone you want to install it on, then follow the on-screen steps to add it to the home screen.

⚠️ Important: this only works if the page is open directly in the Safari app — not in the little in-app browser you get from tapping a link inside Messages, Mail, Slack, or similar apps. Those often hide "Add to Home Screen" entirely, even though they look like Safari.

Safest way to be sure:

  1. Tap Copy Link below.
  2. Open the Safari app yourself (the blue compass icon on your home screen).
  3. Tap the address bar at the top and paste the link in, then go.

Once the page is open in Safari:

  1. Tap the Share button in the Safari toolbar (bottom of the screen).
  2. Scroll down the list and tap "Add to Home Screen." (If you don't see it at all, you're still not in real Safari — see the warning above.)
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
